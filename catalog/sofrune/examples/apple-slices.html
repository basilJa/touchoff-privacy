Fresh apple slices

One serving · 2 minutes

This original recipe is also a small example for Sofrune’s URL importer. After importing, review the instructions, map the ingredient to the correct food and enter its edible weight. Imported text alone does not qualify a recipe for automatic planning.

Ingredient

100 g apple with skin, weighed after removing the core.

Method

  1. Rinse the apple under running water. Remove the core and weigh 100 g of edible apple, retaining the skin.
  2. Slice the measured apple into pieces and serve immediately.
