Sofrune recipes and source states

New original adaptations and English/Turkish editorial copy: Sofrune / basilbase contributors. Other locales are machine-assisted translations, checked for segment counts and numeric data; independent native-language review has not been completed. Recipes are CC BY-SA 4.0. Existing Wikibooks recipes retain their pinned revision and author-history notices.

These recipes have not been kitchen-tested or clinically validated. They are cooking guides, not guaranteed daily nutritional adequacy. Historical labels identify modern adaptations inspired by the application's cited historical context, not reconstructed period menus.

Nutrition uses the exact USDA SR Legacy 2018 records identified by each ingredient's FDC ID, in its stated raw, dry, cooked or ready state. Edible input weights define the calculation. Cooking retention, final yield and optional extras are not inferred. A measured final batch weight can be saved in the app.

Animal-food endpoint guidance: FoodSafety.gov safe minimum internal temperature chart. Times vary with size and equipment; check temperatures with a food thermometer. Store food safely; a user-entered expiry date is not a safety assessment.
