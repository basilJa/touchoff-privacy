ShelfRate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

ShelfRate (software.basilbase.shelfrate) is published by basilbase. Contact: contact@basilbase.com. No account is required and basilbase runs no server for this app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while the Camera screen is open, after you allow it. Each picture is read on the phone by Google ML Kit text recognition; frames are not stored and are not sent to basilbase, Google or the advertising SDK. A photo you pick through Android's photo picker is read the same way; ShelfRate gets access only to that photo and keeps no copy.

Readers for other scripts

If you choose the Japanese, Chinese, Korean or Devanagari reader in Settings, Google Play services downloads that reader once from Google. After that it also runs on the phone. The download contains no pictures, prices or other data from the app.

Exchange rates

To keep rates current, ShelfRate downloads a public daily rate table (about 10 KB) at most every 12 hours while the app is open, or when you tap Update now. The request goes to cdn.jsdelivr.net, with latest.currency-api.pages.dev and open.er-api.com as fallbacks. It contains no information about you, your prices or your conversions; like any web request it reveals your IP address to that server, which handles it under its own policy. You can turn automatic updates off in Exchange rates.

Sharing

When you tap Share, ShelfRate creates one picture (the still with the converted prices) or one line of text and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared otherwise.

Local storage and deletion

Your currency choices, favourites, settings (including tax, card fee and your own rates), the last rate table, your trip budget and the conversions you save to History stay in the app's private storage. Android backup is disabled. Tools → History → Delete all history removes saved conversions; clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes everything. The home-screen widget only shows data already stored in the app.

Permissions

Camera (Live and Snap modes); Internet and network state (rate table and advertising); advertising ID (advertising). Withdrawing camera access in Android settings keeps the converter and gallery photos working. The app reads the country of your mobile network (no permission needed) only to suggest the price-tag currency; it is not stored or sent.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ShelfRate displays a Google AdMob banner. The Google Mobile Ads SDK processes advertising and device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, app and ad interactions, and crash or diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Camera pictures, prices and conversions are never passed to it. Where applicable, Google's consent form lets you choose how advertising data may be used; you can revisit it in Settings → Ad privacy choices and manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Google's advertising data practices.

Security

App data stays in Android's app sandbox. The rate download and the ad SDK use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

ShelfRate is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
