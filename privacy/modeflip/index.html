ModeFlip

Your day, your modes.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.modeflip · Developer basilbase ·contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

ModeFlip switches your phone's settings by mode — for example Sleep, Work or Meeting — when a time, a calendar event, a Bluetooth device, a Wi-Fi network or the charger says so, or when you tap it. When the mode ends it puts back only the settings it changed itself.

Everything ModeFlip reads and stores stays on your phone. ModeFlip does not request the internet permission, so it has no way to send anything over the internet by itself. There is no account, no sign-in, no server of ours, no analytics and no crash reporting. basilbase receives nothing from the app.

Two kinds of data deserve special mention. Android treats the name of the Wi-Fi network you are connected to as location information, so the Wi-Fi trigger needs the precise location permission — ModeFlip uses it only to read that network name, never to find out where you are. And when a mode is set to read text aloud, ModeFlip hands that text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, which is a separate app.

Why the internet claim is checkable

  • The permission list is fixed. Every build of the project reads the final merged manifest of the release and fails if any permission other than the ones listed below appears. INTERNET is on that check's forbidden list, together with background location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS and accessibility.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting library is in the app. Every release build lists its full dependency tree and fails if Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Analytics, AppsFlyer, Sentry, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Segment, Bugsnag, Instabug or Flurry appears, including when another library pulls one in.
  • You can confirm the permission list yourself: on the Play listing under "App permissions", or by inspecting the APK.
  • Without the internet permission, an app cannot open network connections. The other apps that ModeFlip works with (your speech engine, your browser, your email app) are separate apps with their own permissions and privacy policies.

Starred contacts. "Allow starred callers" in Do Not Disturb uses the starred contacts Android already manages. ModeFlip has no contacts permission and never reads your contacts.

Reading text aloud

When a mode that has text to read aloud starts, ModeFlip passes that text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example Google's speech services or your phone maker's engine) through Android's standard text-to-speech interface. That engine is a separate app. ModeFlip only chooses voices the engine reports as working without a network connection; if your engine has no such voice for the app's language, ModeFlip does not speak and says so on the Health screen. Speaking stops after 60 seconds at most. What the speech engine itself does with the text is governed by that engine's own privacy policy.

System Modes on Android 15 and later

On Android 15 and later, each ModeFlip mode is registered with Android as a system Mode rule under the mode's name, which is how ModeFlip applies Do Not Disturb and the grayscale, dim-wallpaper, dark-theme and always-on-display effects. Android shows these rules in its own settings. ModeFlip removes a rule when you delete its mode or use Delete local data. If a ModeFlip rule is still listed in your phone's settings after you uninstall the app, you can delete it there.

Other apps ModeFlip opens

  • App to open by notification: when a mode starts, ModeFlip shows a notification; tapping it opens the app you chose. ModeFlip passes no data to that app.
  • Play / pause media: ModeFlip sends a play or pause key to the media player you used last. No data is sent with it.
  • System panels: shortcuts that open Android's own Wi-Fi, internet, Bluetooth, hotspot, airplane-mode, battery-saver, location, NFC and sound settings for you to change.
  • Privacy policy and contact links open your browser and your email app.

Each of those apps has its own privacy practices.

Backup files you export

Export backup writes one file to a place you choose with Android's file picker. It contains your modes — including names, Wi-Fi network names, Bluetooth device addresses, calendar keywords, the text to read aloud, the package names of apps to open — your theme, language and quick mode, and the wallpaper images you picked. It does not contain recovery records, which modes are active, or system rule IDs, and every mode in it is switched off. Where the file goes is your decision; if you save it to a cloud folder, that service's privacy policy applies to it.

Import backup reads a file you pick, checks all of it in memory, and changes nothing until you confirm. Imported modes start switched off and automation starts paused. ModeFlip holds no storage permission: it can read and write only the files you pick.

Android backup and moving to a new phone

ModeFlip switches Android's own backup off. The app declares allowBackup="false", and its backup rules exclude every storage area both from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. So Android does not copy ModeFlip's data to your Google account or to a new phone. To take your modes with you, use Export backup and Import backup.

Restoring settings, and its limits

When a mode ends, ModeFlip puts back only the settings it changed and still owns. If you changed a setting by hand while the mode was active, ModeFlip leaves your value alone. ModeFlip cannot switch Wi-Fi, mobile data, hotspot, airplane mode, battery saver, Bluetooth, Location or NFC on or off; it opens the system panel for you instead. System Mode effects work only on Android 15 and later. Phones differ: some makers link ring and notification volume, and some stop background apps more aggressively.

If you uninstall ModeFlip, clear its storage, or withdraw Modify system settings or Do Not Disturb access while a mode is active, ModeFlip can no longer restore what it changed, and those settings stay as they are. Use "Stop and restore" first.

Children

ModeFlip is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete a mode in the mode editor. Settings it still owns are restored first.
  • Settings → Delete local data restores the settings ModeFlip still owns, replaces all modes with the six ready-made modes (switched off), resets theme and language, removes ModeFlip's system Mode rules and deletes the wallpaper copies that are no longer needed. The Bluetooth connection record is emptied at the next Bluetooth connection change or restart.
  • Android Settings → Apps → ModeFlip → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling, deletes everything ModeFlip stored on the phone.
  • Exported files are yours; delete them where you saved them.

Because nothing is transmitted to basilbase, basilbase holds nothing about you to access, correct or delete.
