The short version. DoSay turns what you say into text on your phone and carries out the command there. Your voice is never recorded to storage and never leaves the phone. DoSay has no internet permission, no account, no advertising SDK, no analytics and no crash reporting. Notes, lists, reminders and the names you link stay on the phone.

The verifiable version

DoSay's manifest does not request INTERNET. An Android app without INTERNET cannot open a network connection; the operating system refuses. You can confirm this on the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the app bundle.

Speech recognition language packs (Vosk models, Apache License 2.0) are delivered by Google Play through Play Asset Delivery, the same way Play delivers the app itself. DoSay never contacts a server of its own. After a pack is installed, recognition works in airplane mode.

The only third-party code is the Vosk recogniser and Google's AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Material, Room, DataStore and Play Asset Delivery libraries.

Optional special access you may grant in Android settings: the digital assistant role (so the side key or assistant gesture opens DoSay), and notification access (so DoSay can read your notifications and see what media is playing, only when you ask it to; nothing is read aloud on its own). Both can be withdrawn at any time in Android settings.

There is no internet, location, storage, photos, camera, calendar, account, SMS or call log permission, no accessibility service and no usage access.

What DoSay does not do

  • It does not listen in the background. There is no wake word; the microphone opens only after you start DoSay and closes when you stop talking.
  • It does not record, store or send your voice.
  • It does not send what you say, your contacts, notes, lists or notifications anywhere. It cannot.
  • It does not know who you are. There is no account, identifier or advertising ID.
  • It does not read or send SMS or messages in other apps.

What is stored on your device, and only on your device

  • Notes you dictate, and lists with their items.
  • Reminders you set: their text and time.
  • Words you link to contacts (with that contact's name and number) and to apps, and the number you chose last time for a contact with several.
  • Command history, only if you turn it on (off by default): the text of the command, not audio.
  • Settings: answer style, voice and speed, card duration, lock-screen behaviour, speech language, theme and language.
  • Installed language packs.

These live in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The last action is remembered in memory for up to 15 minutes so it can be undone.

Other apps DoSay hands commands to

Alarms and timers go to your Clock app, calls to your phone app, music controls to the app playing music, and “open …” opens the app you name. Those apps handle the request under their own privacy policies; DoSay passes them only what the command needs (for example the alarm time).

Backup

DoSay turns Android backup off. Its data is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Notes and lists can be shared as plain text through Android's share sheet when you choose to.

Children

DoSay collects no data from anyone, children included. It is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.

Data retention and deletion

There is no server, so there is nothing for us to retain. In the app, Settings → Delete all DoSay data removes notes, lists, reminders, linked names and apps and history at once; history alone can be deleted in Settings, single items in their screens. Language packs can be removed in Languages. Uninstalling the app, or Clear storage in Android's app settings, removes everything.
