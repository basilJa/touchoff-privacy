The short version. WageTick does not collect, share or transmit any data. The jobs, hourly rates and work sessions you enter stay on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no advertising SDK, no analytics and no crash reporting.

The verifiable version

WageTick's manifest does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. An Android app without INTERNET cannot open a network connection; the operating system refuses. You can confirm this on the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the app bundle.

There are no third-party SDKs. The app uses Google's AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Material, Navigation, DataStore and Kotlin serialization libraries, which run entirely on the device.

Permissions in full

There is no internet, network state, storage, photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, account, phone or SMS permission, no usage access, no accessibility service, no overlay and no exact alarms.

What WageTick does not do

  • It does not send your rates, jobs or working times anywhere. It cannot.
  • It does not know when you arrive at work or where you are. It counts only between your Start and Stop.
  • It does not know who you are. There is no account, identifier or advertising ID.
  • It does not calculate or report tax, and it shares nothing with an employer or client.
  • It does not show your earnings on the lock screen: there the notification only says that WageTick is counting.

What is stored on your device, and only on your device

  • Each job you add: its name, hourly rate and currency.
  • If a session is running: which job, the rate and currency it started with, and when it started (the device's uptime clock, wall-clock time and restart counter, used to measure time correctly).
  • For each job, the last finished session: its length, the amount, and when it started and ended.
  • Your settings: theme, language, whether the notification is on, whether you swiped it away for the current session, and whether first-run setup was completed.

These live in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

What is deliberately not stored

No list of past sessions beyond the last one per job, no statistics, no usage times and no logs of when the app was opened.

Backup

WageTick turns Android backup off. Its data is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so your pay figures do not leave the phone that way either. On a new phone you start empty.

Children

WageTick collects no data from anyone, children included. It is a general-purpose tool for working people and is not directed at children.

Data retention and deletion

There is no server, so there is nothing for us to retain. In the app, Settings → Delete all data removes every job, session and setting at once and stops a running counter. Uninstalling the app, or Clear storage in Android's app settings, removes everything.

Changes

If a future version ever collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is published. Updated versions are published at this URL with a new date at the top.
