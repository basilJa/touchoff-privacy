Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.saywhen · Developer basilbase · contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

SayWhen speaks the reminders, countdowns, checklists, the time and a morning briefing that you set up, at the times you choose, using the speech engine installed on your phone. Your routines, settings and history stay on your phone.

SayWhen has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. There is no analytics and no crash reporting.

Why that claim is checkable

  • No internet permission. Android does not let an app without the INTERNET permission open network connections. You can confirm the permission list on the Play listing, or by inspecting the APK yourself.
  • Forbidden permissions are checked on every build. Every build of the project reads the final merged manifest and fails if the internet permission, or any of the other permissions SayWhen promises not to use, appears.
  • No tracking code. The app is built from Android's own AndroidX libraries (Jetpack Compose, Core, Activity, Lifecycle, DataStore) and Kotlin coroutines. No analytics or crash-reporting library is included.

The speech engine is a separate app

SayWhen does not contain its own voice. To speak, it hands the words to the text-to-speech engine you have selected in Android settings (for example the engine that came with your phone). That engine is another app with its own privacy terms.

  • What is handed over: the text of the announcement being spoken. Depending on the routine this is your own message, a checklist step, the time and date, the names of people whose birthday is today, today's calendar event titles and times, or the time of the phone's next alarm.
  • SayWhen only uses voices that the engine reports as installed and not needing a network connection. If no such voice exists for a language, SayWhen reports a missing voice instead of using another one.
  • SayWhen cannot see or control what the engine itself does. Some engines can use the internet for some of their features. Check your engine's own settings and policy if this matters to you.

SayWhen keeps no audio recordings and does not use the microphone. It does not keep a copy of your contacts or calendar.

Notifications and alarms other apps can see

  • Alarm icon and next alarm. SayWhen schedules announcements as alarm clocks, as Android recommends for times you set yourself. While an announcement is planned, Android shows an alarm icon in the status bar, and the time of the next announcement can appear wherever Android shows the next alarm (for example a clock widget or the lock screen). Only the time is shared this way, not the words.
  • While speaking, a quiet notification shows "SayWhen" with a Stop button. Its content is hidden on a locked screen. After a checklist starts, a separate notification can say that a checklist is ready; it disappears after 30 minutes. Neither notification contains the words of your routine.

Backup files you make yourself

Backup & restore can write a backup file to a place you choose through Android's file picker, and read one back.

  • The file contains your routines, your settings and the birthdays you entered yourself. Contact birthdays and calendar switches are saved as off. The delivery history, contact data, calendar data and voices are never included.
  • The file is not encrypted. If you save it to a cloud folder, that provider stores and may sync it under its own terms. basilbase never receives a copy.
  • Restoring replaces routines, settings and manual birthdays after you confirm a preview. The delivery history on the phone is kept.

Android backup and moving to a new phone

SayWhen tells Android not to copy its data anywhere:

  • android:allowBackup="false". On Android 8 to 11 this turns off both Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of SayWhen's data. On Android 12 and later it turns off cloud backup.
  • Backup rules for older Android versions and data extraction rules for Android 12 and later exclude every storage area of the app (root, files, databases, shared preferences and external storage) from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer.

A new phone or a reinstall therefore starts empty unless you restore a backup file you made yourself.

SayWhen holds no internet, location, microphone, camera, storage, phone, SMS or accounts permission. It does not ask for Do Not Disturb access, display over other apps, or a battery-optimization exemption; if an announcement was missed or late, Diagnostics can open the battery settings for you to decide. The only other apps SayWhen asks Android to see are installed speech engines.

Children

SayWhen is a tool for households and daily routines; it is not designed for children to use on their own. It collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights and deleting everything

Because nothing is collected or transmitted to basilbase, there is nothing held about you to request, correct or export from us. Everything SayWhen stores is on your own phone.

  • Delete a routine or a birthday in the app.
  • Backup & restore › Delete all app data removes routines, birthdays, settings and history on this phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are kept; delete them yourself.
  • Uninstalling SayWhen deletes all of its data.
