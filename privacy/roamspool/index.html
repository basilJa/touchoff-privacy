RoamSpool

Your own location diary, kept on your phone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.roamspool · Developer basilbase · Contact contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

RoamSpool is a personal location diary. While you have recording switched on, it saves your phone's location and turns it into stops, journeys and gaps. That history stays on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission.

The verifiable version

RoamSpool's manifest does not request INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE or ACCESS_WIFI_STATE; it explicitly removes them. An Android app cannot open a network connection without INTERNET — the operating system refuses. You can confirm this in the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the APK.

That is why the app shows no map and looks up no addresses: a map would mean telling a map server where you are. The day screen draws your route from your own saved points, and places are called what you name them.

What is collected, and when

  • Location, only while recording is on. You switch recording on yourself after RoamSpool explains what it collects. While it is on, the app asks Android for a location at the interval you set (15 to 600 seconds, 60 by default), including when the app is closed or not in use. A notification is shown the whole time, with Pause and Stop.
  • Each saved location holds: time (UTC), the time zone offset the phone used, latitude, longitude, the accuracy Android reported, whether Android marked it as a mock location, the time since the phone started and its restart count (to keep order within one start), and whether it was recorded, imported or added by hand.
  • Nothing is collected while recording is paused or stopped. Pause and Stop stay in effect after the phone restarts.
  • RoamSpool does not request "Allow all the time" location access. When the phone restarts, recording stops; you get a notice, and recording continues when you open the app.

Where the position comes from. RoamSpool uses Android's fused location provider, part of Google Play services. How Google Play services works out a position (satellites, Wi‑Fi, mobile networks) is governed by your phone's location settings and Google's own terms. RoamSpool receives the result on the phone and sends it nowhere.

This is the complete list. There are no others.

Photos

You can attach photos to a stop with Android's system photo picker. RoamSpool sees only the photos you pick; it does not request access to your photo library. It keeps a link to each picked photo and reads its capture time to show whether it was taken during the stop. Photos are not copied, not included in backups and not included in exported files.

What RoamSpool does not do

  • It does not send your location, or anything else, off the device. It cannot: there is no network permission.
  • It does not track anyone else, share your location with anyone, or work as a family or safety locator.
  • It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising ID and no identifier of any kind.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting library.
  • It does not read your contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, call log, messages or the list of installed apps.
  • It does not upload backups anywhere. A backup is a file you save where you choose.

What is deliberately not stored

Backup passwords, usage statistics, device identifiers, crash dumps. Recording-health information is read from Android when you open that screen and is not logged.

Files you create

Exports (GPX, CSV, RoamSpool JSON) and backups are written only when you ask, to the place you choose with Android's file picker. Exports are not encrypted — anyone with the file can read it. Backups are encrypted with a password you choose (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation, AES-256-GCM); RoamSpool does not keep the password, so a lost password means the backup cannot be opened.

Private places in exports. You can mark places as private; locations and stops inside them are then left out of exported files. This reduces what a shared file shows but does not hide your routine completely — times and the edge of the area can still give it away. Backups always contain everything.

Backup and moving to a new phone

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for RoamSpool, so your history is not copied into a Google backup. Moving it is deliberate: make a backup file in the app, then restore it on the other phone.

Children

RoamSpool is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Retention and deletion

Everything stays on your phone until you delete it. In the app you can delete one day or all history (you see how much before anything is deleted), remove single stops, delete places, and set a history limit (off by default) that deletes older history each time the app starts. Deleting inside the app does not touch backup or export files you saved elsewhere. Uninstalling RoamSpool removes its database and preferences.

Limits worth knowing

  • Android and some phone makers stop background apps; recording can have gaps. The app shows gaps and does not fill them with guessed routes.
  • The interval is a request to Android, not a guarantee.
  • Battery use has not been measured across phones yet.
  • Google Play services is required for recording.
