Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.starbend · Developer basilbase · contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

Starbend is a gravity puzzle game. You place planets, moons and black holes, and the game simulates them on your phone. Every mode works offline and there is no account.

The game can send anonymous play statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, but only if you allow it. It asks once, on first start, with no answer selected in advance, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings. Until you allow it, nothing is sent.

What is sent, and only if you allow statistics

When statistics are on and the phone is online, Starbend sends these events to Google Analytics for Firebase:

EventWhat it contains
level_startThe level id, for example "2-5"
level_completeThe level id, the stars earned, and which attempt it was
level_failThe level id, why it was lost (for example "time ran out"), and how many attempts have failed
daily_startThat the daily challenge was started
daily_completeThat the daily challenge was finished, and the stars
sandbox_openThat the sandbox was opened
code_exportThat a system code was copied or shared. Not the code itself
code_importThat a system code was loaded, and whether it worked. Not the code itself

Google Analytics for Firebase also records some things on its own: a random identifier for this installation of the app (the "app instance ID"), the device model, the Android and app version, the language, an approximate location (country, region and city) that Google derives from the network address, when the app is first opened or updated, which screens are shown, and when sessions start and how long they last. Google states that Analytics does not log or store the IP address itself. This list is Google's own description of its product: https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11582702

What is never sent:

  • Your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or location from the phone's location services.
  • The advertising ID. Starbend removes Android's advertising-ID permission from the app and turns advertising-ID collection off.
  • Where the app was installed from. The install-referrer and ad-attribution permissions are removed as well.
  • Anything you type, including system codes.
  • Anything at all while statistics are off.

The purpose is to learn which levels are too hard or too easy, and whether players come back to the game.

Why these claims can be checked

  • Statistics are off until you choose. The app ships with Firebase Analytics collection disabled. It is switched on only by your choice in the first-start screen or in Settings.
  • The permission list is fixed. Every build reads the final merged manifest and fails if a permission other than the four listed below appears, or if the advertising-ID, install-referrer or ad-attribution permission comes back.
  • The libraries are known. Apart from Android's own AndroidX libraries, Kotlin and the libGDX game engine, the app contains Firebase Analytics and Google Play's in-app review library. Firebase Analytics brings in further Google libraries, among them the advertising-ID client and Android's ad-services client; Starbend removes the permissions they need and turns advertising-ID collection off. The app contains no crash-reporting library.

Backup

Your progress and the last sandbox system are included in Android's own backup, so a new phone restored from your Google account can keep them. That backup is made and stored by Android and Google under your account; basilbase never receives it. Settings and your statistics choice are not backed up.

All four are granted at install; Starbend asks for no permission while you play. It has no location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, phone or accounts permission, and uses no accessibility service.

Who receives the data

Statistics go to Google, which runs Google Analytics for Firebase for basilbase as a service provider, under Google's terms for that service. Google may process them on servers outside your country. basilbase sees them only as counts in the Google Analytics console. basilbase does not sell or share them with anyone else.

Google keeps this event data for the retention period set in basilbase's Google Analytics settings, which is 2 months. If that setting changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children

Starbend is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Settings › Share anonymous statistics turns sending off at once. Nothing more is sent after that.
  • Statistics are tied only to the random app instance ID, not to your name or account. basilbase therefore cannot find "your" records on request. If you uninstall the app, the ID goes with it, and data already sent is deleted by Google when the retention period ends.
  • Uninstalling Starbend deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated text is published at the same address before the change reaches a released version of the app.
