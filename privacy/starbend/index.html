Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.starbend · Developer basilbase · contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

Starbend is a gravity puzzle game and a 2D space sandbox. Everything happens on your phone.

Starbend has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. There is no analytics and no crash reporting.

Why that claim can be checked

  • No internet permission. Android does not let an app without the INTERNET permission open network connections. You can confirm the permission list on the Play listing, or by inspecting the APK yourself.
  • The permission list is fixed. Every build of the project reads the final merged manifest and fails if a permission other than the one listed below appears, the internet permission included.
  • The libraries are known. Apart from Android's own AndroidX libraries, Kotlin and the libGDX game engine, the app contains Google Play's in-app review library. No analytics or crash-reporting library is included.

Backup

Your progress, the last sandbox system and your saved systems are included in Android's own backup, so a new phone restored from your Google account can keep them. That backup is made and stored by Android and Google under your account; basilbase never receives it. Settings are not backed up.

The review card

After you solve a level, Starbend may ask Google Play to show its rating card, at most once. The card is shown by the Google Play app, and whatever you rate or write goes to Google Play under Google's terms. Starbend does not receive it and cannot see whether you rated.

Starbend asks for no permission while you play. It has no internet, location, contacts, storage, microphone, camera, phone or accounts permission, and uses no accessibility service.

Children

Starbend is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Saved systems can be deleted one by one in My systems.
  • Uninstalling Starbend, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • basilbase holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request from us.

Changes to this policy

If what Starbend stores or sends ever changes, this policy changes first: the date at the top changes with it, and the updated text is published at the same address before the change reaches a released version of the app.
