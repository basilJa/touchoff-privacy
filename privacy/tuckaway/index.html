TuckAway

Save it now. Read it offline. Listen to it later.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.tuckaway · Developer basilbase ·contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

TuckAway saves the articles you give it, keeps them on your phone and reads them aloud with your phone's own voice. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. There is no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

TuckAway does use the internet, for one purpose: to download the pages you ask it to save, and to show the pages you open in its built-in browser. Those requests go straight from your phone to the website concerned. Nothing is sent to basilbase, because basilbase has nothing to receive it.

Why that claim is checkable

  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library is in the app. Every build of the project runs a check over the full dependency list and fails if Firebase, Google Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, AppsFlyer, Facebook, Sentry, Amplitude, Mixpanel or Segment appears, including when another library pulls one in quietly.
  • The permission list is fixed. Every build reads the final merged manifest and fails if any permission beyond the ones listed below appears.
  • You can confirm the permission list on the Play listing, or by inspecting the APK yourself.

Signing in to a website. The built-in browser exists so that a newspaper you subscribe to can show you an article it would show you in Chrome. What TuckAway deliberately does not keep: no history of what you read when, no statistics, no usage counts, no list of the apps on your phone, no location, no contacts.

The clipboard is read only at the moment you press "Paste and save", and what was read is shown to you before anything is saved. It is never read in the background.

Listening

Articles are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, which is a separate app (for example Google's speech services). TuckAway hands it the sentence to speak. TuckAway chooses only voices that are already downloaded and work without a network; if your engine has no such voice for an article's language, TuckAway tells you which language is missing and does not read the article in another voice. What the speech engine itself does is governed by that engine's own privacy policy.

Backup and moving to a new phone

TuckAway uses Android's own backup. If backup is switched on for your Google account, Android copies the library database and your settings to your Google account's backup. basilbase never receives this copy. It deliberately does not include the article text, the pictures, the saved pages, the search index or website cookies: Android limits each app's backup to 25 MB, and over that limit it backs up nothing. After you restore onto a new phone, TuckAway downloads the article text again and rebuilds the search index by itself.

A direct phone-to-phone transfer (cable or Wi-Fi during setup) has no such limit and also copies the article files.

Export. You can export your whole library at any time, to a folder you choose, as a ZIP file: the articles, pictures, tags and highlights, plus a spreadsheet of links that other read-later apps understand. TuckAway writes only to the folder you pick and holds no storage permission.

Children

TuckAway is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights and deleting everything

Because nothing is collected or transmitted to basilbase, there is nothing held about you to request, correct or export from us. Everything TuckAway stores is on your own phone. Uninstalling TuckAway deletes all of it; a copy in your Google account's backup is managed and deleted there, under your Google account's settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the updated text is published at the same address before the change reaches a released version of the app.
