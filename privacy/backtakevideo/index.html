Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026 · App software.basilbase.backtakevideo · Developer basilbase · contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

BackTakeVideo keeps recent footage in memory during a camera session you start. Choose a duration from 10 to 60 seconds. Tap Save during the session to keep up to the duration you chose as an MP4 clip.

BackTakeVideo processes video and optional audio on your device. It has no internet permission, accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. basilbase does not receive recordings or other app data.

Camera and optional microphone

Tap Start camera and allow camera access to begin. The camera runs only while its preview is visible and the app has focus. If you leave the camera screen, switch apps or lock the phone, the session ends and the app drops unsaved footage.

The microphone is off by default. If you turn on sound and allow microphone access, the app uses the microphone during that same visible camera session. If sound is unavailable or the camera and microphone cannot synchronize reliably, the app can still save a silent clip.

What stays on your device

Saving writes an MP4 to BackTakeVideo’s private storage. The file keeps the video orientation. The app uses the clip’s saved time to list it and makes its thumbnail locally from a frame in that clip. The app also remembers your capture duration, theme and language selection.

Android’s backup and device-transfer rules exclude BackTakeVideo’s private files and settings.

Share, copy and delete

You can share a clip through Android or save a copy to the gallery or a location you select in the file picker, depending on your Android version. The receiving app or provider handles that copy under its own policies. Deleting a clip in BackTakeVideo removes the app’s copy. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove its remaining local data. Manage copies that you shared or exported at their destinations.

Permissions

  • Camera (CAMERA) lets you start a camera session. You can revoke it in Android settings and still open previously saved clips.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) lets you include sound when you choose that option. You can refuse or revoke it in Android settings and still use the camera without sound.
  • Internal broadcasts use an app-specific AndroidX permission that limits which apps can send them. This permission does not give the app access to your personal data.

Children and policy updates

BackTakeVideo is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. If this policy changes, we update the date and the text at this address.
