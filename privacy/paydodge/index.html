The short version. PayDodge does not collect, share or transmit any data. The salary and hours you enter stay on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no advertising SDK, no analytics and no crash reporting.

The verifiable version

PayDodge's manifest does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. An Android app without INTERNET cannot open a network connection; the operating system refuses. You can confirm this on the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the app bundle.

There are no third-party SDKs. The app uses Google's AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Material and DataStore libraries, which run entirely on the device.

Permissions in full

PayDodge asks you for no permissions. The installed app contains exactly one permission entry, added automatically by the AndroidX core library:

EntryWhat it is
software.basilbase.paydodge.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION A signature-level permission private to PayDodge itself. It stops other apps from sending internal broadcasts to it. It is never shown to you and gives no access to any data.

There is no internet, network state, notification, storage, photos, camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, account, phone or SMS permission, no usage access, no accessibility service, no foreground service, no alarms and no boot receiver.

What PayDodge does not do

  • It does not send your salary, hours or break times anywhere. It cannot.
  • It does not know when you actually work, where you are, or who your employer is.
  • It does not keep a history of breaks. Only the result of the most recent break is kept.
  • It does not know who you are. There is no account, identifier or advertising ID.
  • It does not calculate or report tax, and it shares nothing with an employer.

What is stored on your device, and only on your device

  • Your net salary amount, whether it is monthly or yearly, and the display currency.
  • Your normal weekly hours and extra weekly hours.
  • If a break is running: the moment it started and the hourly rate it uses.
  • The result of your last break: its length, the amount, the currency and when it ended.
  • Whether the first-run setup was completed, your chosen theme and your chosen language.

These live in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

What is deliberately not stored

No list of past breaks, no statistics, no usage times and no logs of when the app was opened.

Backup

PayDodge turns Android backup off. Its data is excluded from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so your salary figures do not leave the phone that way either. On a new phone you enter them again.

Children

PayDodge collects no data from anyone, children included. It is a general-purpose tool for working adults and is not directed at children.

Data retention and deletion

There is no server, so there is nothing for us to retain. In the app, About → Delete all data removes your salary, hours and break data at once. Uninstalling the app, or Clear storage in Android's app settings, removes everything.

Changes

If a future version ever collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is published. Updated versions are published at this URL with a new date at the top.
