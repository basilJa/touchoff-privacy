Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026 · App software.basilbase.saytape · Developer basilbase · Contact contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

The short version

SayTape records audio when you tap Record and turns it into text on your phone. Your recordings, transcripts, bookmarks and settings never leave the device through SayTape. There is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. SayTape has no internet permission.

The verifiable version

SayTape's manifest does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE; it explicitly removes them. An Android app cannot open a network connection without INTERNET — the operating system refuses. You can confirm this in the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the APK.

Speech models are downloaded once by the Google Play Store app through Play Asset Delivery, not by SayTape. Google Play handles that download under Google's own privacy policy; SayTape sends nothing with it.

Permissions in full

This is the complete list. There are no others.

Permission Why Without it Withdraw
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) To record when you tap Record. Asked the first time you tap Record, not before. Nothing can be recorded. Opening existing audio files still works. Settings › Apps › SayTape › Permissions › Microphone
Foreground service, microphone type (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE) So a recording keeps going with the screen off or while you use other apps. A notification is always shown while the microphone is on. Recording would stop when you leave the app. Stop the recording
Foreground service, media processing and data sync types (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC) To keep transcribing on the phone after you close the app, with a progress notification. "Data sync" is only the Android 10–14 type for this local file processing; nothing is synced anywhere. Transcription would stop when you leave the app. Pause from the notification
Post notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), Android 13+ To show recording controls, transcription progress and "transcript ready". Recording still works; the controls are not visible in the notification shade. Settings › Apps › SayTape › Notifications
Keep awake (WAKE_LOCK) and run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Added by Google's Play Asset Delivery library (through Android WorkManager), which schedules the speech-model download and resumes it after a restart. SayTape's own code never starts at boot and never records or transcribes at startup. The model download could not be resumed after a restart. Not user-switchable; uninstalling removes them

SayTape does not request storage permissions: recordings are saved through Android's media store into your Recordings (or Music) folder, and files you export go only where you choose in the system file picker.

What SayTape does not do

  • It does not record unless you tap Record (or the SayTape widget), and never starts recording by itself, on a schedule or when the phone starts.
  • It does not upload, sync or share audio, transcripts or anything else. Sharing happens only when you use the Share button, and goes to the app you pick.
  • It does not record phone calls; during a call Android gives the microphone to the call and SayTape marks the silence.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, calendar, phone, SMS or accounts, and does not request those permissions.
  • It contains no analytics, attribution, telemetry or crash-reporting SDK.
  • It has no login, no account and no cloud storage.
  • It does not use your recordings or transcripts to train any model.

App private storage is readable only by SayTape. Deliberately not stored: usage statistics, location, device identifiers.

Backup

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for SayTape. To keep a copy of your transcripts, export them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a recording in SayTape: it moves to the trash; emptying the trash deletes the audio file, its transcript and marks.
  • Remove a speech model: Speech models › Remove.
  • Uninstalling SayTape deletes all app private storage (transcripts, settings, imported copies, models). Recordings in your Recordings or Music folder are your files and remain until you delete them.

Children

SayTape is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If SayTape's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy is updated before that version is published and the date above changes.
