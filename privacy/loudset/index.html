LoudSet

Volume that follows the app you open.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026 · App software.basilbase.loudset · Developer basilbase · contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

LoudSet changes your phone's one shared system volume when an app you chose comes to the front, and puts the previous level back when you leave that app. Media volume is changed by default; ringtone, notification and alarm volume change only if you select them in a rule. Everything LoudSet needs for this stays on your phone.

LoudSet has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. There is no analytics and no crash reporting.

Why that claim is checkable

  • No internet permission. Android does not let an app without the INTERNET permission open network connections. You can confirm the permission list on the Play listing, or by inspecting the APK yourself.
  • The permission list is fixed. Every build of the project reads the final merged manifest and fails if any permission beyond the ones listed below appears, the internet permission included.
  • No tracking code. The app is built from Android's own AndroidX libraries (Jetpack Compose, Core, Activity, Lifecycle, DataStore) and Kotlin coroutines. No analytics or crash-reporting library is included.

About the device key and the output identifier. LoudSet does not write a Bluetooth hardware address to storage. It writes a one-way SHA-256 hash of it (the first 128 bits). That hash is still a fixed value derived from the address, so it identifies the same device every time; it is used only to recognise your device on your phone and never leaves it. An output without a hardware address is identified by a temporary connection number instead.

Learn the new level is off by default. If you turn it on and the level changes outside LoudSet while a configured app is open, the new level is saved into that app's rule.

What LoudSet deliberately does not keep

  • No usage history: no list of which apps you opened, when, or for how long. No statistics and no counts.
  • No log of volume changes.
  • No list of the apps on your phone, only the package names you chose for rules.
  • No location, contacts, accounts, files, microphone or camera data.
  • No notification content. Do Not Disturb access does not let LoudSet read notifications, and LoudSet has no notification-listener access.

Backup and moving to a new phone

LoudSet tells Android not to copy its data anywhere:

  • android:allowBackup="false". On Android 8 to 11 this turns off both Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of LoudSet's data. On Android 12 and later it turns off cloud backup.
  • Data extraction rules, which Android 12 and later read, exclude every storage area of the app (root, files, databases, shared preferences and external storage) from both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • The recovery record is kept in Android's no-backup folder, which Android leaves out of backups.

A new phone or a reinstall therefore starts with no rules. basilbase never receives any copy.

Children

LoudSet is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights and deleting everything

Because nothing is collected or transmitted to basilbase, there is nothing held about you to request, correct or export from us. Everything LoudSet stores is on your own phone.

  • Delete a rule from that app's rule screen.
  • Settings › Clear rules and settings removes all rules and settings. LoudSet refuses this while a level is still waiting to be restored, so that it does not forget a level it still has to put back.
  • Uninstalling LoudSet deletes all of its data. Tap Stop and restore first: an app being uninstalled gets no chance to restore, so the last level can stay in place.
