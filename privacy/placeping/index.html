PlacePing

Reminds you at the place. Tells you when it cannot.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026 · App software.basilbase.placeping · Developer basilbase · Contact contact@basilbase.com · Türkçe

PlacePing alerts you when you arrive at a place you saved, or when you leave it. Your places, reminders and checklists stay on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no internet permission.

The verifiable version

PlacePing's manifest does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE; it explicitly removes them. An Android app cannot open a network connection without INTERNET — the operating system refuses. You can confirm this in the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the APK.

This is why there is no map, no address search, and no way to resolve a shortened map link inside the app. Those are not missing features; they are the cost of an app that cannot talk to anyone about where you are.

How the location part actually works

PlacePing does not watch your position. It hands Android a list of circles — the places you saved, with their radius — and Android wakes the app when you cross one of them. That mechanism is the system's geofencing service, part of Google Play services.

The app therefore never receives a stream of your positions and never stores one. It asks for a single position fix in exactly one situation: when you tap Use my current position while creating a place. That coordinate is written into that place and nothing else.

There is no continuous tracking mode, no foreground service and no "your location is being used" persistent notification, because there is nothing running that would need one.

What PlacePing does not do

  • It does not send your location, or anything else, off the device. It cannot: there is no network permission.
  • It does not record where you have been. No location history, no trail, no visit log.
  • It has no account, no sign-in, no identifier of any kind and no advertising ID.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting library.
  • It does not read your contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, camera, call log or the list of apps installed on your phone.
  • It does not draw over other apps and does not use accessibility services.
  • It does not upload a backup anywhere. The backup is a file you write and keep.

Nothing else. The activity log deliberately holds no coordinates.

What is deliberately not stored

Location history, movement traces, timestamps of where you were, usage statistics, session counts, device identifiers, crash dumps.

Backup

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so your places are not copied into a Google backup. Moving your data to another phone is deliberate: Settings → Export to a file, then Import on the other phone. The file is plain JSON, readable by you.

Children

PlacePing is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Retention and deletion

Everything lives on your phone until you delete it. A place, a reminder or a checklist item is deleted from inside the app; the activity log is cleared on the Reliability screen. Uninstalling PlacePing removes the database and all preferences with it.

Limits worth knowing

These are limits of Android, not choices, and they are written in the app as prominently as the features:

  • An alert can be a few minutes late. Android usually detects an arrival within two minutes, and up to about six when the phone has been still.
  • The smallest radius is 100 metres.
  • Precise location and "Allow all the time" are both required.
  • Manufacturer battery managers can stop reminders; a force-stopped app stays stopped until you open it again.
  • At most 100 active places at once.
  • Google Play services is required.
