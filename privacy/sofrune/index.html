Sofrune Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Sofrune is published by basilbase. Contact: contact@basilbase.com. This policy covers the Android application software.basilbase.sofrune.

What Sofrune does

Sofrune plans meals and snacks, keeps a pantry and grocery list, and records food intake and estimated nutrients. It processes the information you enter on your device. There is no account or remote personal-record service. A public recipe catalogue is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your diet choice; excluded foods and allergen groups; favourite recipes; meal and household counts; planning/shopping interval; cooking-time preference; food prices and budget; editable nutrient targets; pantry quantities and dates; meal plans; shopping amounts and check marks; completed shopping identifiers and last-shopping date; food diary entries, nutrient snapshots and reversible stock movements; and language/theme choices. Diet preferences and food diary information can be sensitive health-related information. This information is used locally to provide the features you select.

Records are saved in the app's private Android storage using DataStore. The app does not implement its own additional encryption layer. Device security depends on your device and operating-system settings. A starter food and recipe catalogue is bundled with the application. Downloaded recipes, their sources and nutrient data are stored in a separate private catalogue file. Update-check preference and last successful check time are stored locally. Retired recipe editions are retained locally to keep your existing plans and intake records readable.

Collection, sharing and permissions

The release application uses Android's INTERNET permission to download the same public recipe catalogue for everyone over HTTPS. The fixed catalogue request does not include your profile, allergies, pantry, prices, nutrient targets, diary, advertising ID or account identifier. Update checks run when opening the app, at most once per day after a successful check; you can disable them in Recipes from the internet. You can also update manually. Previously downloaded recipes work offline. There is no scheduled background-download service.

GitHub Pages and its delivery infrastructure necessarily receive your IP address, the requested catalogue URL, request time and normal connection information, including a generic Sofrune catalogue user-agent. We do not add cookies, personal query parameters, authentication or analytics. Hosting providers may process and retain request information for delivery and security under their own policies; this information is not guaranteed to be erased by deleting app data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Recipe-source websites are not contacted automatically by the app; source links open only when you tap them. Public recipe adaptations are published under CC BY-SA 4.0, separate from your private records.

The app has no advertising, analytics, remote crash-reporting, account, cloud-sync or health-platform integration SDK. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, general storage, notification, accessibility, overlay or health-sensor access.

Alongside INTERNET, the merged release manifest contains the app-specific signature permission software.basilbase.sofrune.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. AndroidX uses it to protect internal dynamic receivers. These permissions do not show runtime permission prompts or grant access to your health information or other apps.

Links you choose to open

When you tap a YouTube or web recipe search, Sofrune hands the selected recipe title and a recipe-search phrase to the external application or browser. Source, privacy-policy and contact links also open only on your action. These external services can receive the address/query and process normal browser or account information under their own policies. Sofrune does not control their data practices or receive their browsing history. It does not automatically attach your full diet profile, pantry or food diary to a search.

If you choose to email support, your email service sends the message you write to basilbase. We use support correspondence to respond to your request; sending email is optional and separate from the app's on-device records. Avoid including sensitive information you do not want to share.

What is not recorded or sent

Sofrune does not read other apps, your browsing history, contacts, photos, microphone, location or device health records. It does not automatically send usage statistics, advertising identifiers, food records or crash logs. It does not operate a remote copy of your diary, an online profile or an account-deletion service because there is no app account.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local records remain until you remove individual records, use Help & about → Delete all my data, clear the app's storage through Android, or uninstall the app. The in-app deletion action removes preferences, stock, plans, shopping records, diary entries and the downloaded recipe archive, and cannot be undone. basilbase cannot remotely access or erase these local records. Clearing data does not delete messages you separately sent through email or records held by an external browser, hosting provider or service.

Android cloud backup is disabled. The application's backup and data-extraction rules exclude its data from cloud backup and device transfer. Your device manufacturer or separate device-management tools may provide their own copying mechanisms; consult those tools before transferring a device.

Adults and nutrition information

Sofrune is intended for adults and is not designed for children. Nutrient values are estimates from the bundled USDA SR Legacy data; brands, preparation and cooking losses can differ. Editable example targets are not a diagnosis, medical prescription or calculation of your personal requirements. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Seek qualified professional advice for medical dietary needs, pregnancy, allergies or other individual needs. Product labels and safe food-handling practices still matter.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy and its date will be updated to match. Contact contact@basilbase.com with questions about this policy or support correspondence.
