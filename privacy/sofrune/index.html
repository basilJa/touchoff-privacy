Sofrune Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · expanded development version

Sofrune is published by basilbase. Contact: contact@basilbase.com. This policy covers the Android application software.basilbase.sofrune.

Your local kitchen

Sofrune connects meal planning, recipes, pantry, shopping, cooking and a nutrition diary. Basic use needs no account. We do not operate a public Sofrune family server. Family sharing is an optional connection to a compatible HTTPS server you or your household choose.

Private Android storage holds your diet, allergies, exclusions, targets, favorites and recipe annotations; household members and participation; custom foods, units, recipes and source credits; plans, meal events and templates; stock, dates, prepared portions and waste history; shopping lists, quantities, checks, prices and currencies; frozen diary nutrient records and reversible stock movements; cooking progress, timers, reminder settings and display preferences. Diet, allergies and nutrition records may be sensitive health-related information.

Most local records use DataStore without an app-specific encryption layer; device protection depends on Android and your settings. Family connection tokens, group keys, pending connection details and offline sharing queue use a separate Android Keystore encrypted file in device-only no-backup storage. They are excluded from portable app backups.

Recipe instructions are not bundled. A small searchable index and food metadata are separate from full details. Only recipes you open, favorite or use in a plan are fetched and cached. Existing historical references are preserved. An uncached recipe needs internet. Optional food photographs use a bounded disposable cache and are off by default. Photos are illustrative; source and license credits are shown.

Online requests you choose

Native catalogue search and preference filtering run on your device. Catalogue requests send a public index, selected recipe or photo URL, normal connection metadata and generic app user-agent to GitHub Pages and its delivery infrastructure. They do not attach your typed native search, diet, allergies, pantry, targets, prices or diary. A plan downloads its selected recipe details, not the whole recipe book. Turning online access off stops new native requests; an already issued request may finish. Saved details continue offline.

When you import a recipe URL, the app requests the selected public HTTPS page, including any path or query you supplied. It retains source/author and an explicit license link where available. An imported recipe remains a draft until you review its food/weight mappings and instructions. No allergy, nutrient or reuse permission is inferred from an arbitrary page. Browser or YouTube searches open only when you choose them and receive the recipe title or search text for that action.

Barcode decoding and receipt text recognition happen on your device. Selected images and receipt text are not uploaded by Sofrune. A product lookup is a separate explicit action that sends the checked barcode to the Open Food Facts public API, with generic user-agent and connection information. Source values are cached; you must review them before saving a custom food. No expiry, food density or purchased weight is inferred from a barcode. OCR language models download from the pinned public tessdata_fast GitHub source only when you choose a language; the file hash is checked. The model provider receives the requested model/language URL and connection metadata, not your image or recognized text.

GitHub and Open Food Facts may retain requests/IP information under their policies. Local deletion cannot erase provider logs. We do not add advertising identifiers, cookies, analytics or remote crash reporting. Provider policies: GitHub, Open Food Facts. External browser, camera, document and sharing apps follow their own settings and policies.

Optional family sharing

Creating or joining a shared household sends end-to-end encrypted plan, pantry, prepared food, shopping lists and preparation tasks to the HTTPS server you select. Household member names, participation, diets, allergies and targets, and required custom foods/recipes/source credits are included. Your own profile targets, personal nutrition diary, prices, private recipe annotations, timers and display settings remain local. A shared meal-consumption flag is separate from your diary: receiving it does not invent a nutrition entry on this device.

The server stores encrypted workspace snapshots and device labels; random household/member identifiers, roles, revisions, invitation expiry and operation/checkout identifiers; hashed access/invitation tokens; and opaque keyed purchase fingerprints used to prevent duplicate checkout. It necessarily receives IP, request times and connection metadata. The group encryption key stays on member devices and in private invitation links; the server does not receive it. Sharing runs while the app is open. Offline edits remain in the encrypted local queue for the next connection.

The owner manages invitations and access. The official app reserves planned meal changes and consumption to the plan creator, lets editors manage shopping/stock and gives viewers read-only shared access. Server role checks enforce membership administration, writer/viewer access and atomic revisions; encrypted individual fields are not separately protected from other members who hold the shared key. Only invite trusted people. Invitations contain the group key, can be used once and expire after 24 hours. Copying one places it on your device clipboard. Send it only through a channel you choose.

Different edits may merge; edits to the same item require a choice. Identical repeated checkout adds stock once. Different purchase details or earlier offline changes to an already completed list require an explicit workspace choice. Local data stays intact until you choose. Revoking a device stops future API access but cannot erase copies or keys it already received. Create a new household with a new invitation/key to establish a new cryptographic membership boundary. Hosting logs and operator backups follow that server operator’s retention practices.

Android permissions and local reminders

INTERNET permits HTTPS requests. POST_NOTIFICATIONS is requested when you enable reminders or add a timer; you can deny it or change channel settings. SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM is optional special access for precise kitchen timers. Without it Android may delay timers. Daily shopping, expiry and preparation reminders use the time you select and may be delayed by system restrictions. RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED reschedules enabled reminders after restart/unlock. There is no remote push, foreground tracking service or full-screen alarm.

Notifications use private lock-screen visibility with generic public text; your Android settings control the final display. Photo Picker grants access only to images you select. An optional system-camera intent uses a temporary app-private capture file; Sofrune requests no CAMERA or broad media/storage permission. Cooking keeps its visible screen awake without adding WAKE_LOCK permission. The AndroidX signature-only dynamic-receiver permission protects internal receivers. Sofrune requests no location, microphone, contacts, accessibility, overlay or health-platform access.

Backups, sharing, retention and deletion

My data lets you export and inspect/restore a user-selected document. Portable backups are checksummed, not password encrypted, and can contain sensitive local records and cached recipe/source data. Choose a private destination. If you select a cloud document provider or share a list/file through another app, that provider receives the document through your action. Images, OCR models, product-lookup cache and family credentials/keys are excluded. Restoring replaces the reviewed local sections; it does not silently reconnect family access. Delete exported/shared copies yourself wherever you stored them.

Cache cleanup removes disposable data and keeps recipe references needed by your saved records. Delete all my data clears this installation’s app records, recipe/photo/product caches, downloaded OCR models, temporary captures, alarms/notifications and local sharing credentials/queue. It does not delete other devices, exported copies, provider logs or the remote household. Automatic Android cloud backup/device-transfer participation is disabled in the manifest; separate device-management tools can have their own behavior.

In Your preferences → Family sharing, non-owners can Leave the shared household to delete their server membership and token. Owners can Delete the shared household to remove the live encrypted workspace, memberships, invitations and operation/checkout ledger for everyone. Disconnect this device deletes only local connection details and unsent queue, keeping current app records. It does not revoke the remote membership. Household profile information in collaboratively shared records can be removed by the plan creator; other members may keep data they already received.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app, contact the operator of your chosen family server or the household owner to request deletion of your membership or household data. You can also email contact@basilbase.com for help with this request. Include the server address and device display name if known. Do not send an invitation, access token, group key, receipt image or private diary. basilbase does not hold access to independently hosted servers; the operator must verify authority and perform the deletion there. Operator backups may have separate retention. You do not need to reinstall the app to initiate a support request.

Adults and nutrition estimates

Sofrune is for adults, not children. USDA source foods, product labels and user-reviewed mappings are estimates; brands, preparation and cooking losses vary. Missing nutrients remain unknown. Example adult targets are editable examples, not a calculation of your requirements. Recipe adaptations have not been kitchen-tested or clinically validated; machine-assisted translations have not had independent native-language review. Allergen labels, cross-contact checks and safe food handling remain necessary. Sofrune is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat conditions. Seek qualified advice for individual medical dietary needs.

Contact and changes

Support email is optional; the email service you choose sends what you write, and we use that correspondence to respond. Avoid sharing sensitive records unnecessarily. We will update this policy and its date when these practices change. Contact: contact@basilbase.com.
