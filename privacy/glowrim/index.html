The short version

GlowRim does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It has no internet permission. There are no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting. It never reads the text of your notifications. Your settings stay on your device.

The verifiable version

GlowRim's manifest does not request INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. An Android app cannot open a network connection without INTERNET — the operating system refuses. You can confirm this on the Play listing's permission list or by inspecting the APK.

This matters because GlowRim has notification access. Without network access, nothing it can see can leave your phone.

GlowRim declares a targeted package visibility query for launchable apps, so it can list them for you to choose which apps may light up. It does not hold QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

What GlowRim does not do

  • It does not read, store, display or analyse the title or text of any notification.
  • It does not keep a history of notifications, of which apps notified you, or when.
  • It does not store calendar events; the next event is read when the glow screen is shown and kept only in memory while it is visible.
  • It does not read, capture or record your screen, and does not record where you tap.
  • It does not use an accessibility service.
  • It does not unlock your phone or dismiss your lock screen.
  • It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, photos, files or accounts, and does not request those permissions.
  • It contains no attribution, analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDK.
  • It has no login, no account and no cloud sync.
  • It does not change your phone's own always-on display or edge lighting settings.

App private storage is readable only by GlowRim. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Deliberately not stored: notifications, notification history, calendar events, battery history, usage statistics.

Backup

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for GlowRim in its manifest. Nothing the app stores is copied to Google's servers as part of a device backup.

Children

GlowRim is a general-purpose utility, not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Data retention and deletion

There is no server, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. To remove everything GlowRim stored, uninstall it, or clear its storage in Settings › Apps › GlowRim › Storage.

Changes

If a future version of GlowRim ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is published and the change will be described in the app's release notes. Updated versions are published at the same URL with a new date at the top.
